Why UV Studio

Built for one job, done exceptionally well

Not a general UV editor — a focused tool for getting artwork onto screens fast, without touching geometry.

Auto-map, one click

Fit every screen to its content instantly — or map one at a time. Imports keep their own UVs until you ask for more.

Lossless round-trips

Send to Cinema 4D or Blender and back — only UV coordinates travel. Your geometry and materials are never touched.

PSD-aware

Drop a layered PSD and every layer becomes linkable artwork — a thumbnail picker matches layers to screens by name.

Real-time distortion feedback

Checker and stretch-map views show exactly where a projection is distorting, before you send anything back.

Reference geometry

Everything that isn't a screen still imports — dimmed, for spatial context — instead of forcing a stripped-down scene.

Free transform

Grab handles to scale, rotate, and reposition a screen's artwork directly — no manual UV editing required.
