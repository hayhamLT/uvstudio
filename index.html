Four steps, start to finish

From a raw model to mapped screens

No UV editing, no manual unwrapping. Bring geometry in, link artwork, map, send it back.

1

Import

Drop a GLB — or Send a selection straight from Cinema 4D / Blender. Screens are auto-detected by name.

2

Link

Attach PSDs / images. A thumbnail picker matches each layer to its screen by name.

3

Auto-map

One click fits every screen to its content. Tweak rotation, flip, or projection per screen if needed.

4

Send back

UVs land back on your original objects, losslessly — or export a textured GLB.
